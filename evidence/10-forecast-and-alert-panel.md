## Defect found and fixed during the ticket

- **The explanation was requested with the sample setting just chosen, not the one the forecast on screen was computed with** (`10-ui-agreement` A6, "no console errors"). With a zone drawer open, switching 50 to 100 futures made the app send `POST /forecast/explain {zone: 3, samples: 100, expected_version: 25}` before any 100-future forecast for version 25 existed, and the server answered 409 "no forecast is held for that state version". The diagnostic copy of the snippet recorded the request as sent by the app, not by the test. `useExplanation` in `frontend/src/Forecast.tsx` now uses `fc.model.settings.samples`; after the fix A6 passes and A5 is unchanged. Written up in Q10.
- **`07-zones` hidden-field regex predated ticket 10.** Its API check flagged `tests_required` in zone responses of `fixture-staffing`. That field became part of the received patient record in this ticket (`null` until tests are ordered, then the kinds; values seen: `null` and `[]`), so the regex in the snippet no longer lists it; `free_at_min`, `arrival_time` and `hidden` still must not appear. Re-run: 624 zone responses, 0 problems.

## Carry-over completed: forecast settings in the saved record

Ticket 09 criterion 4 left `settings.forecast` null "until ticket 10". `save()` now records `{samples, model_version, horizon_min, tail_min, refresh_every_min, seed, warning_threshold}`; the request model takes `forecast.samples` (default 50), the UI sends its selector, and Load restores it (`10-save-settings` K1 to K7: the record equals the live model settings, a 50-futures record holds 50, a reloaded page restores 100 and the source line says 100 sampled futures, the loaded events equal the saved events, a request without the field records 50). `choices` is still empty until ticket 11. ## Judgement calls and limits

1. **The 0.4 warning threshold and the provisional action per service are working decisions** (Q10). Ticket 18 tunes the threshold on tuning shifts; tickets 11 and 17 replace the provisional action.
2. **The development-shift benchmark measures cost, not accuracy.** Seed 7 normal produced no overall congestion risk above 0 and at most one actionable alert in any of its 144 forecasts. Forecast quality is evaluated in ticket 18.
3. **Only 4 of 29 scanned deadlines were open** (25 read "may be too late"). Both statuses are checked; how early alerts appear is a quality result for ticket 18.
4. **Forecast CPU competes with the replay.** Regression 01 on this machine measured 10x playback at 9.9 sim min/s with forecasts running (the first machine saw 9.49); ticket 20 still has to measure interaction and frame rate under forecast load.
5. **The first screen reads "Forecast: computing"** on each zone button until the first forecast arrives (a few hundred milliseconds); it does not claim a risk.
6. **Console:** one `THREE.Clock` deprecation warning from @react-three/fiber internals, unchanged.
