# PulseAhead 3D MVP: rubric-aligned 16-hour build checkpoints

Status: revised planning target. No implementation, deployment, evidence run, or rehearsal has happened. The new 3D view, received ambulance feed, owner routing, and two extra operational presets increase scope; 16 hours is not a guarantee.

Follow the [active spec](PULSEAHEAD_3D_MVP_SPEC.md) and [22-ticket breakdown](PULSEAHEAD_3D_TICKET_PLAN.md). Each ticket is frontend/backend/state/Playwright MCP together. Build a simple real scene first, not a decorative animation awaiting an engine.

These are elapsed checkpoints, not a claim that 22 tickets each take the same time or that multiple developers are required. Work tickets with completed blockers; do not substitute a half-built frontend or engine for a vertical feature.

## Scope priorities

Required: simple 3D four-zone map and truthful dots, inbound ETAs, details/focus/back, reliable coupled resources, received-only forecasts, six concrete action presets, ownership approvals, urgent/donor checks, no-help behavior, matching 2D/keyboard fallback, measured baselines, deployed synthetic demo, and actual Playwright MCP evidence.

Cut first: realistic hospital assets, collision physics, walking avatars, VR, decorative camera choreography, multiple views, nominal learned models, action combinations, production integrations, and extra unmodeled interventions. Never fake a queue, alert, best action, clearance, or resource release to complete the visual.

Catalog examples beyond the six presets require separate modeling before activation. Command-center escalation is recorded workflow, not an unbounded surge lever. The active spec lists exact mechanisms and assumptions.

## Compute and data gates

Benchmark one full development shift and comparison through Playwright MCP by the forecast checkpoint. Record runtime/latency and estimate all forecast/policy evidence costs. Aim for 14 tuning/28 final shifts; if necessary, lock the balanced seven/seven fallback and identities before final outcomes. Single final shifts per family are descriptive, without invented within-family precision.

Keep two-hour warm-up/eight-hour score settings, received-only independent futures, keyed paired demand, confirmation tail, scored-window truth tail, and continuing background arrivals during cohort follow-up. Retain incomplete stays and actual counts.

## Rubric-first evidence

The supplied Track 1 rubric has five equal 20-point categories. Prepare a concrete artifact for each: user/decision/value, forecast/error/noise evidence, detected/categorized/prioritized action loop, plain cause/trade-off explanation, and working deployed application plus timed pitch.

Do not claim business ROI from synthetic waiting-time improvements. Show patient-minutes/boarding/space use beside staff and diagnostic costs; future hospital calibration remains distinct.

## Deployment and final demonstration

Use an available authorized small hosting target, with a stable entry URL and real engine. Prefer one origin and bound concurrent work; isolate replay sessions and expose restart/failure states. The planning task does not provision or publish resources.

Playwright MCP must check the actual hosted workflow and a clean local fallback. A local build, screenshot, or recording does not establish deployment. Preserve actual saved synthetic results and generated replays for network trouble.

Rehearse the rubric's Phase 1 three-minute pitch/two-minute Q&A and Phase 2 four-minute demo/three-minute Q&A, subject to newer organizer instructions. Lead with the supervisor's decision; show the diagnostic alert, uncertainty, action/owner, delayed effect, and measured comparison. Include a blocked/no-help or stale-data case.

Organizer acceptance of synthetic-only evidence remains unverified. Resolve it externally when authorized; it is not established by this rubric or finished planning.
